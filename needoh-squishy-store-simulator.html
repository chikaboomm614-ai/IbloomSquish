About

Run a pastel squishy-toy shop in first person: order stock from the catalog, carry boxes from the stock room, fill shelves, set prices, then scan items and take payment at the register as customers line up.

Normal Mode starts with a small store and a tight budget. Sandbox Mode gives unlimited money, diamonds and stock so you can build freely. Each mode has its own save.

The NeeDoh product line names refer to Schylling's NeeDoh range; this is an unofficial fan game. All 3D models are original procedural designs, and every price is a fictional in-game value.
